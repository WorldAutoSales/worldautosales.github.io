Population & Energy

Three country-level ratios, side by side: new-car sales per capita, the existing car fleet per capita, and oil consumption per capita. Each metric has its own data coverage and vintage — see the notes below before comparing across tabs.

People per New Car Sold

Lower = more cars sold relative to population (a "hotter" market). Ranked list — bar length is inverted (shorter bar = fewer people per car = hotter market). Click a row to isolate it on the map.

Sales per Capita Map

Shaded by people-per-car-sold in each country's latest available year — darker = more cars sold per person. Hover a country for exact figures. Grey = no data for this metric.

  • Population: World Bank (SP.POP.TOTL), 2024, for 105 countries; Taiwan from its own Ministry of Interior (the World Bank does not track Taiwan separately); France's five overseas departments from INSEE/IEDOM. 110 countries in total.
  • Sales per capita: uses each country's latest full-year total from the "Auto Markets by Country" page — a 2026 full-year run-rate projection where available (flagged "proj." in the tooltip), otherwise the latest complete raw year. 107 of 110 countries have a sales figure; Laos, North Macedonia and Suriname don't have a tracked total anywhere on the site yet.
  • Adult population (15+): total population minus the share aged 0-14 (World Bank SP.POP.0014.TO.ZS, 2024, from UN World Population Prospects; Taiwan 11.72% from its Ministry of the Interior, end of 2024). 105 of 110 countries; the five French overseas departments have no age split yet, so they only appear on the per-person view. 15+ is used because it is the standard age cut that is available consistently for every country; an 18+ cut would need single-year age tables per country.
  • Cars on the road per capita: uses each country's 2024 (or nearest earlier year) fleet size from this site's cars-on-road dataset, which currently covers 50 of the 110 countries — real registry counts for most major markets, modeled/anchored estimates for the rest (see the Cars on the Road page for each country's method). The other 60 countries simply aren't built yet for this dataset and show as no data here, not zero.
  • Oil consumption per capita: total refined petroleum product consumption (not just road fuel — also jet fuel, heating oil, petrochemical feedstocks, etc.), from the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy / EIA via Our World in Data, 2024, converted from terawatt-hours to litres using a blended ~36 MJ/L energy density (an approximation, not a precise per-product conversion). This is not split into petrol vs. diesel — no source publishes that split per country in one consistent place; a real petrol/diesel breakdown would need slower country-by-country secondary sourcing, same as this project already does for a few individual markets.
  • Different vintages are being compared against one population year (2024) — sales figures run up to a 2026 projection, fleet and oil figures are 2024. Treat this as a reasonable approximation, not a perfectly synchronized snapshot.
Population: World Bank, Taiwan MOI, INSEE/IEDOM. Sales: this site's own Auto Markets by Country dataset. Fleet: this site's own Cars on the Road dataset. Oil consumption: Energy Institute / EIA via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0).
