Then & Now — the Motorisation Explorer

Every country, three eras: 2010, 2020 and the latest data on file. X-axis is population (log scale), Y-axis is the ratio for whichever metric tab is selected above (always on a per-person basis), bubble size is the total (cars sold / cars on the road / litres of oil). Press Play to watch markets move — bubbles that race downward are motorising fast; bubbles drifting right are simply growing their population. Only countries with data in all three eras can animate; everyone else is left out of this one chart only.

  • Population: World Bank (SP.POP.TOTL) for 105 countries — "Latest" uses 2024, the 2010/2020 snapshots use the matching historical year from the same series; Taiwan from its own Ministry of Interior (household-registration population, year-end 2010 / 2020 / 2024); France's five overseas departments only have a 2025 figure from INSEE/IEDOM, so they don't appear on the 2010/2020 snapshots. 110 countries in total at "Latest."
  • Sales per capita: "Latest" uses each country's latest full-year total from the "Auto Markets by Country" page — a 2026 full-year run-rate projection where available (flagged "proj." in the tooltip), otherwise the latest complete raw year. The 2010/2020 snapshots use that exact calendar year from the same dataset — 103 of 110 countries have at least one of the two, 73 have both.
  • Adult population (15+) and working-age population (15-64): only available for "Latest" (2024 age-split shares from World Bank / UN World Population Prospects, Taiwan from its Ministry of the Interior) — there's no historical age-split loaded yet, so the per-15-64 and per-adult tabs disable themselves on the 2010/2020 snapshots.
  • Cars on the road per capita: "Latest" uses each country's 2024 (or nearest earlier year) fleet size from this site's cars-on-road dataset (50 of 110 countries); the 2010/2020 snapshots use that model's own value for those exact years, same 50 countries — real registry counts for most major markets, modeled/anchored estimates for the rest.
  • Oil consumption per capita: total refined petroleum product consumption (not just road fuel), from the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy / EIA via Our World in Data — covers 105 countries at "Latest" (2024) and at both 2010 and 2020. Converted from terawatt-hours to litres using a blended ~36 MJ/L energy density (an approximation). This is not split into petrol vs. diesel — no source publishes that split per country in one consistent place.
  • Then & Now bubble chart: only plots countries that have population, the selected metric's total, and continent data for 2010, 2020 and latest simultaneously — fewer countries than the map/rank list above, since every one of the three eras has to be present. A country vanishing from one era to the next in this chart means it dropped a data point, not that its market disappeared.
  • Different vintages are being compared against one population year at "Latest" — sales figures run up to a 2026 projection, fleet and oil figures are 2024. Treat this as a reasonable approximation, not a perfectly synchronized snapshot.
Population: World Bank, Taiwan MOI, INSEE/IEDOM. Sales: this site's own Auto Markets by Country dataset. Fleet: this site's own Cars on the Road dataset. Oil consumption: Energy Institute / EIA via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0).
