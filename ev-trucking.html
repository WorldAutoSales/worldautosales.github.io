Who builds them

The electric truck business does not look like the diesel one: in China, newcomers from construction machinery took roughly 30% of the market in 2025, and trade barely exists — only 3% of electric trucks cross a border (25% for electric cars).

Every market

  • Source of the numbers. IEA Global EV Data Explorer, Global EV Outlook 2026 (CC BY 4.0), pulled from its public API: electric vehicle sales, stock and shares for trucks, vans, buses and cars, 2012–2025. "Electric" means battery-electric + plug-in hybrid + fuel-cell. Battery-electric made up 97% of electric truck sales in 2025; fuel-cell trucks are a small niche (about 23,000 on the road, all in China).
  • What is a truck. The IEA counts medium and heavy trucks above 3.5 tonnes (freight trucks). Vans are light commercial vehicles; buses cover city and coach models. The US figures are mostly medium-duty delivery vehicles (over 95% of 2025 US electric truck sales).
  • Who is on the map. The IEA reports 21 countries individually for trucks; the US and India are not published as separate truck lines (North America sold about 20,000 electric trucks in 2025, Canada 2,700; India about 800). Countries in grey are not "zero": they are simply inside regional totals.
  • Rounding. The IEA rounds to two or three significant digits, so shares and units are approximate and small countries can show the same figure in two years. Shares are percent of new sales, not of the fleet.
  • Out of 100 trucks. World and country dots use the IEA sales share; the rest is shown as "diesel & other" because the IEA does not split the conventional remainder by fuel. China's split (electric above 25%, natural gas 15%) is from the IEA text; the EU split is ACEA's first-half-2026 registrations (electrically chargeable 4.8%, diesel 92.1%).
  • Oil figures. Oil displaced by EVs (1.7 mb/d in 2025; electric trucks in China more than 10% of it; 1.3–1.5 mb/d for trucks and buses in 2035) are the IEA's. "About 10,000 litres per truck" is simple arithmetic on those figures (0.17 mb/d over roughly 970,000 Chinese electric trucks) and is a floor, since the IEA says "more than 10%".
  • Manufacturers. The IEA publishes market shares by manufacturer group only as a chart; this page quotes its text (incumbent top-5, new entrants, battery supply, trade). No free source gives unit sales for each electric truck brand.
  • Sources: IEA Global EV Outlook 2026 · ACEA via electrive, H1 2026 · ICCT, zero-emission trucks in China.
Electric truck, van, bus and car data: IEA Global EV Data Explorer (CC BY 4.0). EU registrations: ACEA. Map boundaries: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain).
