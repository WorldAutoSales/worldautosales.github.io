    Non-BEV Cars on the Road

    Estimated non-battery-electric fleet (petrol, diesel, hybrid combined), 2018–2041 — built by aging every year's new registrations with a survival curve and anchoring to real observed fleet-by-fuel data where it exists. Solid = observed or estimated from real fleet data. Dashed (2027 onward) = projected: total new registrations are held constant at the 2026 level, while the non-BEV share of those registrations keeps shrinking at the same compound annual rate observed from 2024 to 2026 for this country — this is a trend continuation, not a guaranteed outcome.
