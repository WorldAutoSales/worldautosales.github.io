Who sells what, country by country

Pick a period and, optionally, one manufacturer or, with the Model filter on top, one model (each can be set against a second one for a head-to-head; the model data, about 24 MB, loads the first time you click the Model box). The map shades each country by that manufacturer's sales (or by total market size when "All manufacturers" is selected). Hover a country to see its top 10 manufacturers for the period, split into BEV, PHEV and HEV + ICE + Diesel. The Segment lane narrows everything to one or several car classes (mini to executive, SUV small or large, MPV, pickup, van; assigned per nameplate from the EU segment letters) and/or powertrains.

How to read this page

Coverage: about 50 countries with brand-level data (monthly from January 2025 where available, yearly back to 2008 for the longest series). Countries drawn in beige have no manufacturer breakdown on this site. Where a source publishes no powertrain split for a brand, its sales are counted as HEV + ICE + Diesel (and a few countries publish totals only). Hong Kong's registry folds hybrids and plug-in hybrids into petrol, so its PHEV share shows 0% even though some plug-in hybrids are in the grey bar; its BEV share is exact. Yearly periods use each country's yearly series; the running year (2026) is offered as "(Projected)" — each brand's year-to-date total through the latest month loaded for that country, annualized to a full-year run-rate (year-to-date × 12 / months loaded) — rather than a raw partial total, so it's comparable to the complete prior years in the same list. Every brand has one permanent color taken from its logo (Toyota red, Ford blue, BMW silver…) and each country carries a badge with its winner's logo; logos come from the open-source simple-icons set, so brands without one (BYD, Perodua, Stellantis, VinFast…) show their initials instead. "Compare / Split switches above the continent and country charts redo them for BEV only or non-BEV only (PHEV counts as non-BEV), or cut every bar into BEV and non-BEV. For a manufacturer or parent group with a worldwide total in its financial reports (annual reports, 10-K / 20-F, official year-end sales releases; full years only), the charts add a "Reported worldwide" row and a hatched "Unclassified" row: reported sales minus the sum of the tracked countries, i.e. markets without brand data here, differences of basis (reported wholesale or deliveries vs. registrations) and joint-venture brands that this site files under another owner (Wuling under SAIC, for instance). The reported figures, regions and sources are in the Supabase table manufacturer_fin_report_sales. Parent group" attributes each brand to its owner as of the selected year (e.g. Volvo and Polestar count under Geely, not Ford, from 2010 onward).
