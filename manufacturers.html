How to read this page

Coverage: about 50 countries with brand-level data (monthly from January 2025 where available, yearly back to 2008 for the longest series). Countries drawn in beige have no manufacturer breakdown on this site. Where a source publishes no powertrain split for a brand, its sales are counted as HEV + ICE + Diesel (and a few countries publish totals only). Hong Kong's registry folds hybrids and plug-in hybrids into petrol, so its PHEV share shows 0% even though some plug-in hybrids are in the grey bar; its BEV share is exact. Yearly periods use each country's yearly series; a year that is still running (2026) is year-to-date through the latest month loaded for that country, so countries can end on different months. Every brand has one permanent color taken from its logo (Toyota red, Ford blue, BMW silver…) and each country carries a badge with its winner's logo; logos come from the open-source simple-icons set, so brands without one (BYD, Perodua, Stellantis, VinFast…) show their initials instead. "Parent group" attributes each brand to its owner as of the selected year (e.g. Volvo and Polestar count under Geely, not Ford, from 2010 onward).
