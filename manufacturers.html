Who sells what, country by country

Pick a period and, optionally, one manufacturer. The map shades each country by that manufacturer's sales (or by total market size when "All manufacturers" is selected). Hover a country to see its top 10 manufacturers for the period, split into BEV, PHEV and HEV + ICE + Diesel.

How to read this page

Coverage: about 50 countries with brand-level data (monthly from January 2025 where available, yearly back to 2008 for the longest series). Countries drawn in beige have no manufacturer breakdown on this site. Where a source publishes no powertrain split for a brand, its sales are counted as HEV + ICE + Diesel (and a few countries publish totals only). Yearly periods use each country's yearly series; a year that is still running (2026) is year-to-date through the latest month loaded for that country, so countries can end on different months. "Parent group" attributes each brand to its owner as of the selected year (e.g. Volvo and Polestar count under Geely, not Ford, from 2010 onward).
