Country Stats

Growth is battery-electric (BEV) registrations, comparing each country's latest available month to 12 and 36 months earlier. "Sold" figures use that same latest month, falling back to the 2026 full-year projection if a country has no valid month at all. Ranks are always against every tracked country, regardless of the sidebar filters.

  • Norway is a massive outlier — its BEV share is so far ahead of every other market that a straight linear color scale would leave the rest of the map looking flat. The map colors are bucketed by rank (quantiles), not raw percentage, so the spread across the other 30 countries stays readable.
  • "EV %" here means battery-electric (BEV) only. Plug-in hybrids (PHEV) and full hybrids (HEV) are shown as their own segments in the stacked bars below, not folded into the map's green.
  • Canada joined in Q1 2026. Statistics Canada reports quarterly, not monthly like Europe's ACEA source — its quarterly total is split evenly across the quarter's 3 months rather than fabricating a real monthly breakdown, and its "latest month" trails Europe's until a newer quarter is published.
  • New Zealand joined in 2023-01 (through July 2026 — the site's most current market). It's not an independently-published monthly series like the others: it's reconstructed from NZTA Waka Kotahi's Motor Vehicle Register snapshot, filtered to new (not used-import) passenger cars/vans and grouped by first-NZ-registration month and fuel type. This can slightly undercount older months as vehicles are later exported or scrapped (checked as negligible over this window) and was cross-checked against MIA's independently reported June 2026 figure (0.3% difference). Most of Asia, Africa, South America, and the rest of Oceania are still unsourced; the grey countries aren't zero, they're just not sourced yet.
  • United States joined in 2023-01 (full history through July 2026, no gaps) — source is Argonne National Laboratory's monthly light-duty-vehicle sales updates (Wards Auto-sourced), the same organization behind this site's annual US figures. Argonne doesn't publish a Petrol/Diesel split for the US market, so the entire non-BEV/PHEV/HEV remainder is shown as Petrol (Diesel=0) — diesel's real share of US passenger-vehicle sales is negligible in any case.
  • China joined for 2026-01 through 2026-07 only (no pre-2026 monthly data sourced yet) — figures are CAAM's own monthly wholesale sales (automaker deliveries to dealers, including exports), matching the wholesale basis already used for this site's existing China annual figures, via CnEVPost's monthly CAAM write-ups. China's official "NEV" category is BEV+PHEV only — non-plug-in HEV sales are counted within the conventional/ICE segment, so HEV is shown as 0 and folded into Petrol along with the (unpublished, believed negligible) Diesel share.
  • Mexico joined with full history 2023-01 through 2026-07, no gaps — source is INEGI's RAIAVL open data (Registro Administrativo de la Industria Automotriz de Vehículos Ligeros). No Petrol/Diesel split is published for the Mexican market, so the non-BEV/PHEV/HEV remainder is shown as Petrol (Diesel=0).
  • Brazil joined with full history 2023-01 through 2026-07, no gaps — source is ANFAVEA's monthly "Carta da Anfavea" newsletter. Brazil's dominant Flex-fuel (dual gasoline/ethanol) vehicles are folded into Petrol, since the schema has no separate flex-fuel bucket and ethanol is itself a petrol-substitute in Brazil's flex system.
  • Argentina joined but only for scattered 2025-2026 months (2025-05, and 2026-03 through 2026-07) — source is ACARA/SIOMAA's monthly "Informe de Mercado 4W" report, specifically its fuller "_Instit" tier which carries a combined hybrid table; that tier is only publicly available for a handful of recent months, not a continuous series, so most of the timeline shows as a gap rather than a fabricated figure. HEV here is a combined HEV+MHEV+PHEV figure (the source doesn't split further at monthly cadence) and Diesel is shown as 0 (Petrol carries the ICE remainder).
  • Japan joined with full history 2023-01 through 2026-07, no gaps — source is JADA's monthly fuel-type-by-manufacturer workbooks. Registered passenger cars only (登録車) — excludes kei-cars (roughly 35-40% of Japan's new-car market) and commercial vehicles, so Japan's totals aren't directly comparable to other countries' whole-market figures.
  • Indonesia joined for 2026-01 through 2026-07 only, no pre-2026 monthly split sourced yet — GAIKINDO doesn't publish a machine-readable monthly feed, so these figures were cross-assembled from multiple press outlets that each cite GAIKINDO's own wholesale releases for a single fuel category per article; Diesel/Others are shown as 0 (not published at this granularity).
  • Malaysia joined with full history 2023-01 through 2026-07, no gaps — source is data.gov.my's open vehicle-registration dataset (JPJ), aggregated by month. PHEV is folded into HEV (Malaysia's registrations data doesn't separate them at this granularity).
  • India joined with full history 2023-01 through 2026-07 (real monthly data actually reaches back to 2011 and forward through August 2026, beyond this page's current MONTHS window), no gaps — source is the Vahan Dashboard (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways), filtered to the Four Wheeler category via its public analytics API. India has a large CNG passenger-car segment; since the schema has no dedicated CNG column, it's folded into Others along with LPG, ethanol, and other minor fuel types.
  • Turkey joined for full-year 2024, May 2025, and 2026-01 through 2026-07 — 2023 and most of 2025 are real gaps, not fabricated. Source is ODMD's (Otomotiv Distribütörleri ve Mobilite Derneği) monthly "Basın Bülteni" press bulletins, using each bulletin's own single-month fuel-type table (not its year-to-date column). Scope is passenger cars (Otomobil) only, excluding light commercial vehicles, so Turkey's totals aren't directly comparable to other countries' whole-market figures. Others is Otogaz (LPG-converted vehicles), a real and non-trivial category in the Turkish market.
  • Thailand joined for 2025-01 through 2026-07 (no pre-2025 monthly powertrain split sourced yet) — source is the FTI Automotive Industry Club's (สภาอุตสาหกรรมแห่งประเทศไทย) monthly domestic sales briefings via mreport.co.th. Total is the light+medium vehicle market (passenger cars/SUVs/MPVs plus pickup trucks/PPV/5-10 ton medium trucks), not passenger-cars-only, so Thailand's totals aren't directly comparable to other countries' whole-market figures. Diesel is a residual (commercial-segment total minus explicitly-reported electrified commercial units each month), so a small amount of unreported commercial BEV/HEV/REEV volume in any given month is folded into it undetected.
  • Singapore joined for 2026-01 through 2026-07 only, no pre-2026 monthly data sourced yet — source is LTA's (Land Transport Authority) own "New Registration of Cars by Make" open workbook, aggregated across every make/importer type/car type. Singapore's new-car market is small and deliberately capped by its COE (Certificate of Entitlement) quota system, so volumes aren't comparable in scale to other countries' whole markets. Figures include COE and tax-exempted cars but exclude taxis.
  • South Africa joined for 2026-04 through 2026-06 only — source is naamsa (National Association of Automobile Manufacturers of South Africa). The series intentionally starts in April, not January: BYD only began reporting its sales to naamsa from March 2026, so earlier months would understate the true BEV market. April and May's BEV/PHEV/HEV split is an even 50/50 divide of a derived two-month combined figure, since naamsa hasn't published a standalone powertrain breakdown for either month individually — an approximation, not two independently measured months. June is a genuine standalone naamsa figure. Diesel is folded into Petrol (no separate split published for new-energy vs conventional vehicles).
  • Nepal joined for 2026-01 through 2026-07 only — source is the Department of Customs (customs.gov.np), Chapter 8703 (motor cars) import data by 8-digit HS subheading. This is import data, not registrations — accepted as a close proxy since Nepal has negligible domestic vehicle production. Three-wheelers/auto-rickshaws are excluded from every figure (cars/SUVs/vans/pickups only). Nepal's fiscal year runs on the Bikram Sambat calendar, which never lines up with Gregorian months, so Nepal doesn't publish a "January" or "February" figure directly — each calendar month here is a day-weighted blend of the two adjacent Nepali fiscal months that overlap it (e.g. "January" blends 14 days of Poush and 17 days of Magh), derived by subtracting the government's own cumulative year-to-date snapshots. Nepal also has one of the highest BEV shares in the world (hydropower-driven tax policy strongly favors electric over combustion imports) — its high percentages here are real, not a data error.
  • Kazakhstan joined for 2026-01 through 2026-06 only — source is KAO (Qazaqstannyñ Avtokölik Odağy / Kazakhstan Automobile Union, kao.kz), an industry association founded in 2023. Total is passenger + light commercial vehicles combined (KAO doesn't publish a separate passenger-only total most months). BEV and PHEV (PHEV here includes EREV range-extenders, not split further) come from KAO's dedicated "NEV market" reports, published roughly every two months as year-to-date cumulative snapshots rather than true single months — 2026-01/02, 03/04, and 05/06 are each a genuine 2-month delta (subtracting consecutive snapshots) divided evenly across their pair of calendar months. HEV (classical non-plugin hybrid) has a real total-market figure for January-February only (107 units); from March onward, KAO's reports only list top-brand leaders (Toyota/Lexus/Porsche) without a full total, so HEV is a floor for those months, not a true measurement — user's explicit choice over guessing or omitting it. Diesel is folded entirely into Petrol (no separate figure published or estimated).
Sources: ACEA (European Automobile Manufacturers' Association, EU + EFTA + UK, refreshed monthly) · Statistics Canada, Table 20-10-0025-01 (refreshed quarterly) · Argonne National Laboratory (United States, refreshed monthly) · CAAM/CPCA via CnEVPost (China, refreshed monthly, 2026 only) · INEGI RAIAVL (Mexico, refreshed monthly) · ANFAVEA (Brazil, refreshed monthly) · ACARA/SIOMAA (Argentina, scattered months only) · JADA (Japan, refreshed monthly) · GAIKINDO via press outlets (Indonesia, 2026 only) · data.gov.my / JPJ (Malaysia, refreshed monthly) · Vahan Dashboard / MoRTH (India, refreshed monthly) · ODMD (Turkey, passenger cars only, 2024 + May 2025 + 2026 YTD) · FTI Automotive Industry Club via mreport.co.th (Thailand, refreshed monthly, 2025-01 onward) · LTA (Land Transport Authority) (Singapore, refreshed monthly, 2026 only) · naamsa (South Africa, refreshed monthly, since April 2026) · Department of Customs Nepal (imports, 2026 only) · KAO (Kazakhstan, bimonthly NEV snapshots, 2026 only)
