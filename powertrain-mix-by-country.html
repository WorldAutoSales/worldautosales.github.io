Powertrain Mix by Country

New car registrations by powertrain (Battery Electric, Plug-in Hybrid, Hybrid, Petrol, Diesel, Others) across 31 European markets. Countries ranked by BEV (battery-electric) share. Use the filters to zoom into a powertrain, country, or month.

📝 Notes
  • Norway leads the world in EV adoption by a wide margin — battery-electric alone made up over 97% of May 2026 registrations, a market so far along the transition that petrol and diesel are now a rounding error.
  • Some of the largest EU markets (Germany, France, Italy) still sit in the middle of the pack once hybrids are counted — hybrid-electric (not battery-electric) is the single most popular powertrain in most of Europe right now.
  • A handful of Eastern European markets (Latvia, Romania, Bulgaria) remain almost entirely petrol/diesel, showing how uneven the EV transition still is within the EU itself.
